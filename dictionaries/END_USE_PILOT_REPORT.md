## Soap Manufacture (`oe:ind_soap_manufacture`)
19th-century hard, soft, yellow, mottled, curd, Castile, and toilet soap manufacture — combining animal tallow and tropical/vegetable oils with caustic alkalis (barilla, kelp, soda ash, potash, lime), rosin, common salt for salting out, and essential oils for scenting.

## Calico Cloth & Calico Printing (`oe:ind_calico_printing`)
19th-century cotton weaving, bleaching, dyeing, and calico printing — combining raw cotton with bleaching powder and alkalis, natural, insect, mineral, and coal-tar dyestuffs (madder, garancine, munjeet, alizarine, indigo, cochineal, lac dye, kermes, logwood, quercitron, fustic, brazil wood, safflower, orchal/archil, persian berries, prussian blue, chrome yellow, aniline), metallic mordants (alum, copperas, tin/lead/copper salts, sumach, galls, cutch), and thickeners (gum senegal, gum arabic, tragacanth, starch, British gum, flour, albumen).
