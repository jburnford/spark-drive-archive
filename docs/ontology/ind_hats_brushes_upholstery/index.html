Hat-Making, Brushes, Upholstery & Turnery

Felt and silk hat-making, brushmaking, upholstery stuffing, and ornamental turnery — working beaver, rabbit/coney, and nutria pelts with shellac stiffening, nitrate of mercury carroting, and logwood/copperas black dye; hog bristles, horsehair, piassava, coir, and kapok; and ivory, whalebone, tortoise-shell, mother-of-pearl, cork, and rattan.
