VEGETABLE CHARCOAL: charred or burnt wood, which is largely used for fuel on the Continent, and is also valued for making glass, steel, and as a deodorizer when powdered; cylinder charcoal obtained by distilling non-resinous woods is used in the manufacture of gunpowder. See CARBON.
