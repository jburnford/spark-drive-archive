verbatim text of the passage
BEES-WAX: the wax-comb after the honey is removed, which is used for making candles, sealing-wax, polishing furniture, &c. We import quantities from India, Africa, and other parts of the world. Two kinds occur in commerce, the yellow and the white or bleached.
