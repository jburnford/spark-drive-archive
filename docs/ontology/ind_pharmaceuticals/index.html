Pharmaceuticals & Druggists' Preparations

19th-century apothecary, pharmacopoeia, and fine-chemical drug manufacture (e.g., Howard & Sons, Allen & Hanburys) — extracting alkaloids and preparing tinctures, pills, plasters, and ointments from cinchona/quinine, opium/morphia/laudanum, coca/cocaine, nux vomica/strychnine, quicksilver/calomel, ipecacuanha, jalap, senna, rhubarb, aloes, camphor, castor/cod-liver oils, and iodine.
