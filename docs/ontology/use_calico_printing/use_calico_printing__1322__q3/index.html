verbatim text of the passage
As a bleaching reagent chloride of lime is extensively employed, such, for example, as in the raising of metallic colours, in discharging Turkey reds, and in calico-printing, etc. In India lime is made into a resist paste with ordinary gum-arabic, and in that form is utilised by the calico-printer.
