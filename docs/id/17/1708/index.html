Bowsprits

a kind of mast, resting slopewise on the head of the main stem, and having its lower end fastened to the partners of the foremast, and farther supported by the fore-stay. It carries the sprit-sail, sprit-top-sail, and jack-staff; and its length is usually the same with that of the fore-mast. [Thomas Mortimer (rev. Dickinson), A General Commercial Dictionary (1823), scan p. 172]
