Wallaba

the *Eperua falcata* of Aublet, a tree abundant in British Guiana, the wood of which is of a deep red colour; it is hard and heavy, but splits freely and smoothly, and is much used for shingles, staves, pallings, posts, house-frances, &c. The wood may be cut 30 or 40 feet long, and 15 to 20 inches square. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 416]
