Hartshorn

rasplings from the horn of the deer, which are used medicinally. When boiled to a jelly it is nutritive, and a pungent volatile spirit was formerly obtained by distillation from the horn, but it is now made from carbonate of ammonia and similar products of bone. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 201]
