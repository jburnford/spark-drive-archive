Coriander

an umbelliferous plant, the *Coriandrum sativum*, grown for its pungent fruit, used as a seasoning in India, and for the essential oil expressed from the seeds, which are a warm and agreeable aromatic. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 119]
