Acacia

(GER. *Acaciensaft*. FR. *Suc d'acacia*. IT. *Acacia*. SP. *Acacia verdadera*, *o'de Levante*. PORT. *Acacia*. LAT. *Acacia succus*.) Egyptian thorn, a species of *Mimosæ*, from which the Chinese make that yellow which we see bears washing in their silks and stuffs, and appears with so much elegance in their painting on paper. [Thomas Mortimer (rev. Dickinson), A General Commercial Dictionary (1823), scan p. 12]
