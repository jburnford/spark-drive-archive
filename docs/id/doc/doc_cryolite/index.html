Cryolite

a mineral only found on the west coast of Greenland, lately imported as a source of aluminium; which it is said can be afforded at a price as low as silver. Besides this metal and crystals of soda, a clay is obtainable which will be valuable to calico printers as a substitute for a compound of alum and sugar of lead. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 127]
