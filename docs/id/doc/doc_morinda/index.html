Morinda

A genus of erect or climbing shrubs or trees which comprises about 40 species, all tropical. Of these seven are natives of India. *M. angustifolia*, Roxb. ; *Rec. Bot. Surv. Ind.*, i., 248, 347. The *dáru haridrá*, *asugach*, *chenung*, *ban hardi*, *yiyo*, etc. An evergreen bush of the Eastern Himalaya ascending the hills to 6,000 feet in altitude ; Assam ; [Sir George Watt, The Commercial Products of India (1908), scan p. 794]
