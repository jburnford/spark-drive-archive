Malachite

or _mountain green_, is native carbonate of copper of a beautiful green colour, with variegated radiations and zones; spec. grav. 3·5; it scratches calc-spar, but not fluor; by calcination it affords water and turns black. Its solution in the acids, deposits copper upon a plate of iron plunged into it. It consists of carbonic acid 18·5; deutoxide of copper 72·2; water 9·3. [Andrew Ure, A Dictionary of Arts, Manufactures, and Mines (Project Gutenberg #44562)]
