Star Anise

the *Illicium anisatum*, a native of China and Japan, the aromatic and carminative fruit of which is used in the preparation of liqueurs. The star-shaped capsules and seeds abound in an essential oil, easily procured by distillation with water. See **ANISEED**. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 371]
