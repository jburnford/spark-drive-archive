Gentian

Gentian root comes from the genus of flowering plants, Gentiana. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gentiana) Gentian is used to treat a wide range of ailments in the Compendium, including gout, scrofula, worms, dyspepsia, epilepsy, and jaundice. [Danika Drugs Project: British Pharmacopoeia (1867) / Savory (1865)]
