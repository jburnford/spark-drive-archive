Myrobalans

The dried fruits of different varieties of *terminalia*. The fruit, varying from the size of an olive to that of a gall-nut, consists of a white pentangular nut, covered by a substance about 2 lines in thickness. The latter, which is the only valuable part, is mucilaginous and highly astringent; [J. R. McCulloch, Dictionary of Commerce and Commercial Navigation (1880), scan p. 999]
