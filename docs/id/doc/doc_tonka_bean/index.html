Tonka Bean

the fruit of the _Dipterix odorata_, affords a concrete crystalline volatile oil (_stearoptène_), called _coumarine_ by the French. It is extracted by digestion with alcohol, which dissolves the stearoptène, and leaves a fat oil. It has an agreeable smell, and a warm taste. It is fusible at 122° Fahrenheit, and volatile at higher heats. [Andrew Ure, A Dictionary of Arts, Manufactures, and Mines (Project Gutenberg #44562)]
