Caviar

a preparation of the dried spawn or salted roe of fish; a single sturgeon will sometimes yield as much as 120 lbs. of roe. Caviar is principally consumed in Russia, Germany, and Italy, by the Greeks during their long fasts; and also in England. The black caviar is made from the roe of the sturgeon; [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 89]
