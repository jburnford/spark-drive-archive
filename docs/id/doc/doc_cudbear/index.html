Cudbear

A purple or violet coloured powder used in dyeing violet, purple, and crimson, prepared from a species of lichen (*Lecanora tartarea*), or crustaceous moss, growing commonly on limestone rocks in Sweden, Scotland, the north of England &c. About 130 tons of this lichen are annually exported from Sweden. It commonly sells in the port of London for about 20*l.* per ton; [J. R. McCulloch, Dictionary of Commerce and Commercial Navigation (1880), scan p. 555]
