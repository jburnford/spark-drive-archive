Chamois Leather

leather made from various kinds of skins, dressed with fish-oil. The oil is hammered or beaten by a mill into the pores of the skin, which is afterwards partially dried, and washed in strong alkali, when it becomes very soft and pliable. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 94]
