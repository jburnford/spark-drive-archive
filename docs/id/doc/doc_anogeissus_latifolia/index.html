Anogeissus Latifolia

The *dhaura*, *dhauri*, *dhavada*, *hesel*, *bákli*, *arma*, *vellay naga*, *chiriman*, etc. A large deciduous pas Tree tree of the Sub-Himalayan tracts from the Ravi eastward to Central and Southern India. Ascends to 3,000 feet in altitude. Dhaura GUM is used in calico-printing and has been suggested as likely to be useful to dyers in England (*Journ. Chem. Soc. [Sir George Watt, The Commercial Products of India (1908), scan p. 82]
