Squill

a name for the *Urginea Scilla* of Steinhell, the *Scilla maritima* of Linnaeus; a plant inhabiting the sea-coast of the Mediterranean, the acrid bulbs of which being diuretic, expectorant, emetic, and purgative, are used medicinally. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 369]
