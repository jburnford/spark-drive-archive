Cardamom

Cardamom is a spice made from the dried seed capsules of cardamom plants. It is used in an assortment of remedies, and is listed in the Compendium as a Stomachic and Cordial. [Danika Drugs Project: British Pharmacopoeia (1867) / Savory (1865)]
