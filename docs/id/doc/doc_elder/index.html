Elder

the *Sambucus nigra*, a tree common in Britain and the South of Europe. The wood, which is remarkable for its hardness, is often used for carpenters' rules, weavers' shuttles, meat-skewers, &c. The light pith of the branches is used for electrical purposes. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 151]
