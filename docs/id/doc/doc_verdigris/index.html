Verdigris

the acetate of copper; obtained by exposing thin plates of copper, for some time, to the action of the air or more of the wine-presses in the South of France. Besides the French verdigris, there are green distilled, common, and crystallized verdigris. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 411]
