Bassia Butter

a name given to the solid oil from *Bassia butyracea*, sometimes called Galam butter. Another species, *B. latifolia*, furnishes the Mohwa oil of Bengal, and the Shea butter is obtained from *B. Parkii* in Africa. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 41]
