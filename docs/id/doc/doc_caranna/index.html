Caranna

is a tenacious, resinous concrete; while fresh, it is ductile like pitch, but hard and friable when it has been kept for any time; outwardly of an ash colour inclining to black; inwardly of an obscure brown. It is brought to us from New Spain and other parts of America in little masses rolled up in leaves of flag. [Thomas Mortimer (rev. Dickinson), A General Commercial Dictionary (1823), scan p. 201]
