Bush Tea

the leaflets of a species of *Cyclopia*, probably *C. latifolia*, native of the Cape of Good Hope, supposed to possess expectorant and restorative properties; a few bags of this drug have been imported into London. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 68]
