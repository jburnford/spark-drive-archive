Hemlock Spruce

the *Abies Canadensis*, the wood of which is not held in much estimation. It has, however, been used for railway sleepers in England, and is much used for laths. The bark is largely used for tanning in the United States, although inferior to oak bark. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 204]
