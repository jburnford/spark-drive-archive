dcterms:description
Four-tier historical archival SKOS vocabulary and thesaurus (9 Macro-Sectors -> 44 Commodity Groups -> Canonical Substance Groups -> 2,066 persistent verbatim British & Canadian customs headings, dual-typed as skos:Concept and crm:E55_Type for LINCS/CIDOC CRM interoperability) enriched with definitions, scopeNotes, synonyms, cross-stage material chains, and verified Linnaean source taxa mined from six 19th-century commercial dictionaries (Simmonds 1892/1854, Mortimer 1823, McCulloch 1880, Watt 1908, Ure) and linked via reversible multi-anchor SKOS mappings to Wikidata (2026-11-09), FAO AGROVOC, ZBW STW Economics, Getty AAT, Catalogue of Life, SITC Rev. 4, and Theodoridis (2018).
