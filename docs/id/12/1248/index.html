Charcoal

charred or burnt wood, which is largely used for fuel on the Continent, and is also valued for making glass, steel, and as a deodorizer when powdered; cylinder charcoal obtained by distilling non-resinous woods is used in the manufacture of gunpowder. *See CARBON. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 95]
