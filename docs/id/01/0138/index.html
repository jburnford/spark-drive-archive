Cocoa

the commercial name for the dried seeds or beans contained in the fruit pods of the *Theobroma Cacao*. In commerce these seeds are sold raw or roasted, and crushed and powdered, prepared in the form of roek or flake cocoas; deprived of the husk as cocoas nibs, or sweetened and flavoured made into cakes under the name of chocolate. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 108]
