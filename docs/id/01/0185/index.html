Gum Arabic

a general trade name for several descriptions of clear soluble gums. The best, or true white gum, is yielded by *Acacia verec* of Guilenin, the red gum arabic by *A. Adansonii*: *A. vera* also yields gum arabic and a part of the senegal gum. Our imports of gum arabic are about 3000 tons a year. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 195]
