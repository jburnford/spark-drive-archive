Palm Oil

a dark-yellow oil obtained from the fruit of the oil-palm of the west coast of Africa, the *Elais Guineensis*. It is used with other solid oils for making soap and candles. Our imports amount to about 40,000 tons a year. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 285]
