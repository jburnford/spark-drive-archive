Weld

an annual herbaceous plant, the *Reseda luteola*, a native of Europe, the stems and leaves of which dye yellow. The whole plant is cropped when in seed, at which time its drying power is greatest; and, after being simply dried, it is brought to market. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 421]
