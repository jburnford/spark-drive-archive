htc:1831

Manna

a sweet gummy exudation from several plants: the Calabrian manna is produced in tears from a species of ash, the *Fraxinus rotundifolia*, while some is obtained from another species, the *F. florifera*. The oriental manna of the desert is from *Alhagi Maurorum*. Manna is used medicinally. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 251]
