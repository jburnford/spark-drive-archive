Sulphuric Acid

one of the most important chemical agents used in the arts and manufactures, made by conducting the burning lumps of sulphur through a leaden vessel, exposed to the action of an oxide, water, and steam. It is used in the manufacture of soda-ash, in bleaching woolens, straw, &c., and for a variety of chemical productions, to the extent of 300,000 tons per annum. It is also called oil of vitriol. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 379]
