Gambier

an extract prepared at Singapore from the *Uncaria Gambir*, and used as a dye and tanning substance. It is misnamed in trade circles *Terra Japonica*. The imports in the last few years have averaged 6000 tons per annum. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 180]
