Cotton

the wool or down surrounding the seeds of various species of *Gossypium*, of which the commerce is enormous. The quantities of raw cotton consumed in the chief manufacturing countries in 1856 were as follows, in millions of pounds:—Great Britain, 920; Russia, Germany, Holland, and Belgium, 256; France, 211; Spain, 48; countries bordering on the Adriatic, 39; United States of North America, 265; Mediterranean and other countries, 56. Total 1795 million pounds. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 121]
