Wool

the soft curly hair or fleece covering of sheep—one of the most important of animal fibres, in which the trade, home and foreign, reaches nearly 100,000 tons a year. The kinds of wool raised at home, and received from abroad, are very numerous. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 427]
