Bacon

the cured and smoked flesh of the hog, forming a large article of commerce. Ireland and the United States are the countries where the trade in bacon is most largely carried on. Sometimes, when intended for home use, bacon is simply salted and dried green instead of smoked. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 32]
