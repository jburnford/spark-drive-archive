Maple Sugar

sugar prepared from the sap of the *Acer saccharinum*, which is largely made for home consumption by the settlers in parts of the United States and British North American Provinces: usually it is of a dark treacle colour, as it does not granulate readily; but it is occasionally refined and bleached. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 252]
