Guano

a valuable fertilizer, imported from various quarters, chiefly from Peru, consisting of the excrements of sea-fowl, and the decomposed carcasses of marine animals. In the last sixteen years we have received upwards of two million tons: the average annual imports of the four years ending with 1856, having been 215,000 tons. [P. L.
