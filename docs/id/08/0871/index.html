Argol

an acidulous concreto salt, the crust or sediment of wine vats and casks, which when purified is denominated cream of tartar; it is white or red, according to the wine of which it is the deposit, and largely used in medicine and the arts; also a Tartar name for the dried excrements of animals moulded into cakes and sold as fuel. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 27]
