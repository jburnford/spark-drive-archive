Jute

an annual plant common in Bengal, the *Corchorus olitorius*. There are two varieties of the plant, the green and the reddish, known by the local names of pat and bun-pat. A coarse kind of cloth is woven from the jute, and affords the materials of the well-known guinny-bags of India. See GUNNY-BAGS. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 223]
