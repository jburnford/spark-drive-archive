htc:0627

Tobacco Manufactured Cavendish or Negrohead

species of *Nicotiana*, in which a large trade is carried on in most parts of the world; the leaf being used for smoking, chewing, and, when powdered, inhaled through the nose. The quantity of tobacco imported into the United Kingdom, in 1836, was 44,788,130 lbs., besides two million lbs. of manufactured, and snuff; of this quantity there were entered for home consumption, 32,578,987 lbs. *See SHIRAZ TOBACCO*, and *SYRIAN TOBACCO*. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 394]
