Brimstone

a simple inflammable body, of great importance in chemistry and the arts. It is found abundantly in depositions near volcanoes, and is also obtained by the roasting of pyrites. It is used extensively in the manufacture of gunpowder, and of sulphuric acid. We import annually about 75,000 tons. [P. L. Simmonds, The Commercial Dictionary of Trade Products (1892), scan p. 378]
