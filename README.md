# Open Empire (`spark-drive-archive`): Historical Trade SKOS Vocabulary, Open Corpora & Entity Spine

**Live Interactive SKOS Vocabulary & Visual Tree:** https://jimclifford.ca/spark-drive-archive/

Reproducible code, open public-domain commercial dictionary lexicons, and extracted Layer-1 historical trade data & LINCS-compatible SKOS vocabularies (`skos:Concept` + `crm:E55_Type`) for *London's Ghost Acres* and comparative British World environmental history (University of Saskatchewan HGIS Lab).

## Published identifiers

Each project entity has a citable page on GitHub Pages with embedded JSON-LD and a linked Turtle representation (`entity.ttl`). The [compressed full graph](docs/data/open-empire.ttl.gz) and [VoID dataset description](docs/void.ttl) support bulk reuse. Open a commodity ID in the inspector to visit its record. For example, the permanent identifier for Hops is [Hops (htc:0056)](https://jimclifford.ca/spark-drive-archive/id/00/0056/#concept).

The project tables retain short keys such as `htc:0056`. Use the [identifier crosswalk](docs/identifier-crosswalk.tsv) to translate the previous USask IRIs to the canonical public IRIs. In the RDF and SSSOM exports, the corresponding compact IRI includes its fragment, for example `htc:00/0056/#concept`. The crosswalk records the migration at version 7.0 and should be retained with future releases.

Run `python scripts/build_entity_pages.py` to generate the static pages from the published vocabulary, end-use network, and SSSOM mapping set. This step requires RDFLib and can run offline. GitHub Pages serves the committed `docs/` tree after changes reach the publishing branch.

## Rights & Sharing Policy (Strict 3-Tier Boundary)
1. **Included in this repository (Open Code, Open Documents, & Extracted Data):**
   - **All pipeline code:** Internet Archive / BHL discovery, download, Narval Chandra-2 OCR batching, dictionary parsing, Layer-1 trade consolidation, place/polity grounding, and SKOS v4 ontology generation (`scripts/`, `human_trade/scripts/`).
   - **Tier-1 Open Public Sources:** Audited manifests for public-domain Internet Archive / BHL / Project Gutenberg holdings (`manifests/`) and derived structured lexicons from 6 19th-century commercial dictionaries (`dictionaries/`).
   - **Extracted Archival Data (Layer 1 — `human_transcribed`):** Consolidated extracted observations (`human_trade/layer1_human/human_trade_observations.tsv.gz`), unique printed name tables, place/polity grounding (`places_grounding.jsonl`, `place_year_polities.tsv`), and the 4-tier SKOS v4 commodity vocabulary (`commodities_skos.{ttl,jsonld}`, `commodities_mappings.sssom.tsv`, `commodities_concepts.jsonl`, interactive `open_empire_layer1_skos_v4.html`).
2. **Strictly Excluded (Local / Private Only — enforced via `.gitignore`):**
   - Raw archival reading-room photography and scans (`/mnt/data/jic823/archival_corpus/`, TNA `CUST 4/5/26` PDFs).
   - Raw archival transcription spreadsheets and unpermissioned raw archival source files (`human_trade/raw_sources/`).
   - Licensed/vendor digitized corpora (e.g., Adam Matthew `CO 879`, paywalled JSTOR, `historyofceylontea.com`).
   - Large binary PDFs and raw OCR page dumps (`/mnt/data/jic823/open_empire_corpus/`).
3. **Layer Separation:** Layer-1 (`human_transcribed`) extracted trade records are kept strictly distinct from future Layer-2 (`llm_extracted`) archival OCR extractions.

## Build, Version and Verification
- **Version 7.0** (`MANIFEST.json`): one record of the vocabulary version, namespaces, Wikidata snapshot, curated-table sizes and a SHA-256 for every published file.
- **Verify the published files** without any restricted source: `pip install rdflib && python scripts/verify.py` (file hashes, Turtle = JSON-LD, core ⊂ extended, the end-use graph joins to the vocabulary by IRI, tier filters, clean hierarchy, plausible dates).
- **Rebuild everything** in dependency order: `scripts/build_all.sh` (needs the raw sources and external data roots; override with `OE_WD_SLIM_GLOB`, `OE_OCR_ROOT`, `OE_GUTENBERG_ROOT`). Outputs are deterministic.
- `archive/end_use_pilot/` holds the superseded 3-industry pilot. Do not load it alongside the end-use network.

## Repository Layout
- `SOURCES.md` — Provenance, license/rights tier, and publishability rules for every collection (including the 6 Wave 1b commercial dictionaries).
- `manifests/` — Audited Internet Archive / BHL item catalogs (`wave1_economic_botany.tsv`, `wave1b_commercial_dictionaries*.tsv`, `wave2_colonial_bluebooks.tsv`, `wave3_regional_ghost_acres.tsv`, and discovery snapshots).
- `scripts/` — Discovery, curation, download, Narval Slurm OCR bundling, dictionary parser (`parse_commercial_dictionaries.py`), and binomial grounding (`ground_dictionary_binomials.py`).
- `dictionaries/` — Parsed structured lexicon (`commercial_dictionaries_lexicon.jsonl`, 28,063 entries across 6 19th-century commercial dictionaries) and verified Linnaean taxon grounding (`dictionary_binomials_grounded.jsonl`, 1,193 Wikidata/Catalogue of Life taxa).
- `human_trade/`
  - `scripts/` — Layer-1 consolidation (`build_human_layer1.py`), places/polities grounding (`ground_places_v2.py`), SKOS v4 builder (`build_skos_v4.py`), and interactive HTML inspector builder (`build_skos_html_inspector.py`).
  - `layer1_human/` — Consolidated extracted Layer-1 observations (`human_trade_observations.tsv.gz`, 153,579 rows cleanly partitioned by `row_kind`), `CONSOLIDATION_REPORT.md`, `names/*.tsv`, and `grounding/` (persistent `htc_id_registry.tsv`, `commodities_skos.{ttl,jsonld}`, `commodities_mappings.sssom.tsv`, `places_grounding.jsonl`, `place_year_polities.tsv`, `GROUNDING_REPORT.md`, Opus review reports, and standalone `open_empire_layer1_skos_v4.html`).

## Current Milestones (2026-11-25)
- **6 19th-Century Commercial Dictionaries Parsed & Grounded:** Simmonds (1892), Mortimer (1823), McCulloch (1880), Watt (1908) via Narval Chandra-2 OCR (`198/198` bundles PASS) + Simmonds (1854) & Ure (2nd ed., 1840) from Project Gutenberg → **28,063 entries**, **1,193 verified Linnaean taxa** (parser rewritten in v6.1: inverted sub-entries such as *TOBACCO, MOUNTAIN* are separate headwords, entries no longer run on into the next headword, definitions end at a sentence).
- **Layer-1 Places & Polities Grounding v2:** **93.9%** of 270,080 mentions grounded to verified Wikidata QIDs (**98.5%** resolved with explicit aggregates) + **1,067** year-aware Empire Evolution `IN_POLITY` pairs.
- **Layer-1 Commodity SKOS Ontology (v6, content-reviewed — see `REVIEW_V6.md`):** 4-tier hierarchy (**9 Macro-Sectors → 44 Commodity Groups → 185 Substance Groups → 2,066 persistent `htc:` concepts**), **85.8%** of 152,625 commodity rows (**1,420 concepts / 131,018 rows**; by relation: exact 160 headings / 34,869 rows, close 26 / 3,688, broad 1,205 / 84,310, related 29 / 8,151 — so exact+close cover 25.3% of rows; residual headings cut to **1.92%** of rows) grounded to verified non-taxon Wikidata anchors + composed Getty AAT/STW/AGROVOC URIs (**3,703** SSSOM rows), **424** 19th-century `skos:definition` entries with clickable IA/Gutenberg provenance, **79** `skos:altLabel` sets, **289** concepts with bidirectional `skos:related` links, **11** merged `oe:combinesConcept` headings, cross-stage `oe:materialConcept` chains, and **230** concepts with Linnaean `oe:sourceTaxon` links (0 TTL/JSON-LD triple diff, 0 orphans/cycles). **285** classification/anchor corrections (`curated_overrides.tsv`) and **172** definition/taxon corrections (`curated_field_fixes.tsv`) are applied by the builder, each with a written rationale published as `skos:editorialNote` (classification notes also carry the heading's Layer-1 trade evidence). Facets include `oe:landBasis` (with `terrestrial_wild_hunted` for furs, game, ivory and castoreum) and `oe:harvestMode`, whose `wild_hunted_fished` value joins those wild land animals to the fisheries and whaling. Series attributions that were not transcribed (63,981 rows with a blank series cell) are published as `oe:inferredPrimarySource`, separate from `prov:hadPrimarySource`. Readings that hold only for part of a heading's rows are separate `oe:ScopedReading` nodes (`curated_scoped_readings.tsv`; piloted on *Cocoa nuts*).
- **19th-Century Commodity Text-Mining Gazetteer & Extended Document-Attested SKOS Tier (`htc:doc_*`):** Built specifically for downstream NER/text mining across unenumerated minor commodities (zero quantity estimation), combining Layer-1 (`2,066` `customs_enumerated` concepts) + **394** `document_attested` concepts (`43` from Danika's Drugs Project & 1866 Customs List citing *British Pharmacopoeia* 1867 / Savory 1865 + `351` mined across the commercial dictionaries; **95** Wikidata anchors, **165** with Linnaean taxa; review removed 53 non-commodities, 122 duplicates of existing customs lines and 60 variant spellings — `curated_doc_tier_decisions.tsv`; linked via `oe:subsumedInCustomsHeading` to Layer-1 catch-all headings such as `htc:0122 Drugs Unenumerated`, `htc:0054 Dye Stuffs Unenumerated`, `htc:0506 Chemical Manufactures Unenumerated`, `htc:0220 Gum Unenumerated`). Exported as flat **`commodity_gazetteer.{tsv,jsonl}`** (**3,426** form-concept pairs over 3,259 distinct normalised strings: `prefLabel` + `altLabel` + Latin binomials + curated `text_form` / `source_plant` forms, 13 flagged `ambiguous_alone`; first stress-tested on the Kew Ceylon volumes, see `curated_gazetteer_forms.tsv`), LINCS-compliant **`commodities_skos_extended.{ttl,jsonld}`** (**0 blank nodes**, **0 dangling URIs**, 0 S13 clashes), and searchable live at https://jimclifford.ca/spark-drive-archive/.
- **18-Industry Primary-Source End-Use Network (`oe:industry_scheme`, `oe:usedInIndustry` / `oe:usesCommodityInput`):** Mined across **6,648 full-text pages** of Ure (1840), Simmonds (1854 & 1892), McCulloch (1880), Mortimer (1823), and Watt (1908) — **320** commodity→industry links spanning **198** distinct commodities (`150` Layer-1 customs + `48` Document-Attested) across **18** major Victorian manufacturing industries (*Soap*, *Calico Cloth & Printing*, *Woollen & Silk Dyeing*, *Chocolate & Confectionery*, *Leather Tanning*, *Candles*, *Paper Making*, *Varnish/Paint/Lacquer*, *Glass & Ceramics*, *India-Rubber & Waterproofing*, *Brewing & Distilling*, *Sugar Refining*, *Shipbuilding & Cordage*, *Gunpowder & Matches*, *Perfumery*, *Pharmaceuticals*, *Metallurgy & Tinplate*, *Hat-Making, Brushes & Upholstery*), backed by **573** displayed verbatim quotes with clickable IA/Gutenberg URIs; links are keyword-mined and then hand-reviewed (`curated_end_use_link_decisions.tsv`: 52 unsupported links dropped, 20 re-pointed, 47 with roles corrected or irrelevant quotes removed), so treat unreviewed roles as provisional (`end_use_network_links.{tsv,jsonl}`, `end_use_network_skos.{ttl,jsonld}`, which now shares the commodity vocabulary's namespace so all 198 commodities join to their concepts by IRI; each link also has an `oe:EndUseAssertion` evidence record in the RDF carrying its role, its verbatim passages with citations, and a review status (`reviewed` 68 · `mined_multiple_quotes` 142 · `mined_single_quote` 110 — treat the last as candidates). Ure and Simmonds 1854 quotes are cited by dictionary entry (*s.v.*), since the ebooks have no page numbers, and interactive cross-industry search live on GitHub Pages).
