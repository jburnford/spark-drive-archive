# Ontology review of the Open Empire historical trade vocabulary

25 November 2026 · Review of the working tree on `review-v6-content-fixes`, based on commit `c4302f9`.

**The customs vocabulary is dependable for finding a printed heading and recovering its ledger rows. The most serious failure occurs when a researcher follows that heading into the industrial end-use graph.** All 2,066 registered headings reconcile individually to the observations. All four Turtle files agree with their JSON-LD counterparts. Yet a query joining the commodity and end-use graphs by RDF identifier returns zero commodities, even though the network identifies 198 by their abbreviated IDs. The other urgent issue is historical dating. Two four-digit source values propagate into a last attestation in the year 9601 and two future Canadian polity assignments.

For *London’s Ghost Acres*, I would retain the registered headings as the unit of archival identity. They preserve distinctions historians need to revisit. I would give interpretations of material, land use, time, and industry their own evidence and scope. That would let researchers use a well-supported interpretation for one series or period while leaving another open.

## Strengths worth preserving

**The printed heading survives changes of interpretation.** The registry has 2,066 distinct labels and 2,066 distinct IDs. Excluding 514 QA rows, 168 blank rows, and 272 other rows without a commodity leaves 152,625 rows. Rejoining by the exact printed heading reproduces every concept’s observation count. An analyst can return to the original series, year, route, and unit after a Wikidata mapping changes. That property is more valuable for longitudinal work than maximizing the number of external matches. [ID registry](htc_id_registry.tsv) · [Consolidated observations](../human_trade_observations.tsv.gz) · [Consolidation report](../CONSOLIDATION_REPORT.md)

**The hierarchy and the relationships answer different questions.** The core contains nine sectors, 44 commodity groups, and 185 intermediate substance groups. Of the registered headings, 1,229 pass through a substance group and 837 attach directly to a commodity group. Following ancestors accommodates both. I found no cycles, multiple direct parents, dangling local object IRIs, asymmetric local `skos:related` links, or overlap between those links and a local ancestor. The commodity graphs also passed per-resource preferred-label language cardinality and lexical-label disjointness checks. Each SKOS concept carries the additional CRM E55 type. Separate predicates for biological source, base material, and combined headings let a researcher ask how an article relates to a taxon or material without treating them all as synonyms. [Core Turtle](commodities_skos.ttl) · [Extended Turtle](commodities_skos_extended.ttl) · [W3C SKOS Reference](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/)

**The project records adverse evidence and leaves room for uncertainty.** The reviewed snapshot includes 285 classification overrides, 172 field corrections, and 297 decisions on document-tier candidates. The commodity JSONL distinguishes dictionary-derived, curated, and Wikidata-derived taxa. The core distinguishes exact, close, broad, and related mappings. Whiting, a one-row heading, retains a local concept without a forced external anchor. A researcher can inspect why a classification was adopted and reopen it when better evidence appears. [Classification decisions](curated_overrides.tsv) · [Field decisions](curated_field_fixes.tsv) · [Document-tier decisions](curated_doc_tier_decisions.tsv) · [Concept records](commodities_concepts.jsonl)

**The sources support discovery beyond the ledgers.** Of 395 document-attested records, 383 have definitions, 165 have source taxa, and 95 have Wikidata anchors. The network’s evidence table makes it possible to read the passage behind an industry association. For example, the Simmonds passage attached to Hops and brewing identifies which hops brewers used for different beers. The separate document tier and zero ledger row counts make this material available without inventing trade quantities. [Document-attested records](document_attested_concepts.jsonl) · [End-use evidence](end_use_network_links.jsonl)

## Findings and consequences

### 1. The commodity and industry graphs use different identifiers for the same headings

**Highest priority for linked-data use.** Hops is `htc:0056` in both files. The commodity builder expands it to `https://open-empire.hgislab.usask.ca/vocab/human-trade-commodities/0056`. The network expands it to `https://openempire.hgis.usask.ca/vocab/commodities/0056`. The `oe:` namespace differs as well. All 321 network pairs match their intended commodity by compact ID in the reviewed snapshot. None joins to its scheme member by full IRI. The network emits its own labeled commodity nodes, so a check for missing objects inside that file passes. A graph query for the customs observation count of a brewing input still returns nothing. This also affects all 81 pilot commodity identifiers. [Commodity builder](../../scripts/build_skos_v4.py) · [Network builder](../../../scripts/build_end_use_network.py) · [Pilot builder](../../../scripts/build_end_use_pilot.py)

Use one shared namespace definition and one documented active network. Preserve a migration path for already-circulated identifiers. Test the joint publication by loading the extended vocabulary and active industry graph into the same RDF store and retrieving the registered Hops concept through brewing. The query in the verification section measures the whole network.

### 2. Source-year errors become positive historical assertions

**Highest priority for temporal and polity analysis.** Two Flax Seeds records, `ht_tc2021_090155` and `ht_tc2021_090156`, carry the printed year values 2449 and 9601. Both become `origin_detail` with empty QA flags. The four-digit parser accepts them, the commodity graph publishes `htc:0116 oe:lastAttested 9601`, and the polity table assigns Quebec QC to Canada in both years. The place records for Quebec QC and Great Britain also end in 9601. These outputs magnify two source anomalies across several independent-looking results. A defensible correction requires checking the source. Pending that check, the printed values can be preserved and the derived dates withheld. [Year parser and row classification](../../scripts/build_human_layer1.py) · [Place grounding](places_grounding.jsonl) · [Polity table](place_year_polities.tsv)

Period boundaries create a separate problem. There are 54,219 commodity-bearing rows with different start and end years. The commodity builder takes the maximum **start** year as the last attestation. For 364 headings the maximum recorded end year differs. Planks and Boards ends at 1897 in the graph although its final reporting period runs through 1898. Place-year lookup also uses the start year. Define whether a query asks for the ledger’s reporting year or the interval it covers. Retain both endpoints for interval queries and for periods that cross a political transition. [Commodity builder](../../scripts/build_skos_v4.py) · [Place-year builder](../../scripts/ground_places_v2.py)

### 3. An external anchor is often too broad for the historical question

The headline grounding rate, 85.8% of commodity-bearing rows, is reproducible. It measures the presence of an anchor. The strength of that anchor changes what a researcher can infer.

| Primary Wikidata relation or status | Headings | Ledger rows |
|---|---:|---:|
| Exact match | 160 | 34,869 |
| Close match | 26 | 3,688 |
| Broad match | 1,205 | 84,310 |
| Related match, including compounds | 29 | 8,151 |
| Local concept only | 639 | 18,803 |
| Customs residual status | 7 | 2,804 |

Exact and close together cover 25.3% of ledger rows. Horses and Swine both map broadly to livestock. Grouping by target alone would erase the difference between them. The latest core gives Oats, Barley, Maize, and Rye exact grain anchors, an improvement on the earlier review. Preserve that progress by publishing coverage by relation, heading count, and row count. Also distinguish a residual *classification* (53 headings, 2,924 rows) from the residual *grounding status* in the table above. [Concept records](commodities_concepts.jsonl) · [Previous review](REVIEW_V6.md)

All 3,703 distinct external mapping triples agree between core RDF and SSSOM. The TSV has 3,705 rows because the Basswood, Butternut and Hickory heading (`htc:0139`) emits the same `relatedMatch` to wood three times. The components all lose their distinction at that target. Confidence also has only two values. Every exact lexical match gets 0.95, every other lexical match and all 2,273 chained mappings get 0.85. The `author_label` field holds a snapshot or a Wikidata property name. Those are sources or methods. Keep the authority, method, reviewer, and evidence for each component distinguishable, and describe the scores as rule defaults unless they have been calibrated. [SSSOM export](commodities_mappings.sssom.tsv) · [SSSOM field definitions](https://mapping-commons.github.io/sssom/dev/)

### 4. The model preserves words more precisely than it preserves changing meanings

The scope note for `htc:0843 Cocoa nuts` is a good historical question in miniature. The 25 later Annual Statement rows are recorded by number. The note says all nine earlier rows were recorded by weight. Eight were. The ninth, `ht_tc2021_017422` in 1840, uses “Number.” This does not settle whether the earlier entries refer to cacao or coconuts. It does establish that the source evidence differs from the note. The graph offers one heading-wide coconut association, with the uncertainty confined to prose. Preserve the ID and attach candidate readings to the relevant series, periods, units, and rows. A search for coconut can then distinguish supported rows from unresolved ones. [Scope note and curation rationale](commodities_concepts.jsonl) · [Ledger evidence](../human_trade_observations.tsv.gz)

The same distinction matters for provenance. The builder turns a blank series field into `CSP_Canadian_Exports_BlankSeries`, which enters RDF as a primary source. Among commodity-bearing rows, 63,981 have a blank printed series, affecting 766 headings. The assignment may be well motivated by the source file or trade routes. Record that basis and mark it as inferred. A user then knows which series attributions were transcribed and which were reconstructed. [Commodity builder](../../scripts/build_skos_v4.py) · [Consolidation report](../CONSOLIDATION_REPORT.md)

Two other distinctions survive in the working tables but disappear from RDF. The 1866 bridge records 144 exact-or-synonym readings and 33 broader readings. The RDF exporter targets the historical **broader category**. Wheat, for example, records a historical Wheat entry in JSONL and publishes a broad match to 1866 Corn in RDF. The group-level statement is useful, but a consumer cannot reconstruct the Wheat-to-Wheat evidence from it. Likewise, 128 customs concepts have field-curation explanations in JSONL. Of those, 118 have no RDF editorial note at all. Wheat’s replacement definition is one example. Preserve the historical entry, its parent, and field-level editorial decisions as distinguishable assertions. [1866 and field-curation data](commodities_concepts.jsonl) · [RDF exporter](../../scripts/build_skos_v4.py)

### 5. The land and processing facets support screening more readily than acreage estimates

The core now distinguishes wild-taken terrestrial animals from livestock. Sixty-four headings covering 4,793 rows receive `terrestrial_wild_hunted`, including 185 rows for Beaver pelts. This addresses a consequential problem identified in v6. The rule also applies to generic Furs and Furs Unenumerated Undressed. Those labels alone establish neither the species nor the procurement system. Likewise, the harvest mode follows mechanically from land basis. A second field derived from the first adds a way to group results but supplies no independent evidence about procurement. [Facet rules](../../scripts/build_skos_v4.py) · [Concept records](commodities_concepts.jsonl)

For an acreage analysis, distinguish the upstream biological resource, its possible production systems, and the processing stage of the traded article. Retain “unknown” and “mixed” where evidence permits multiple readings. Keep a separate decision about which rows and units may be added. The 152,625 commodity-bearing rows include 117,030 origin details, 26,688 export totals, 8,867 import totals, and 40 malformed-year rows. An observation count measures archival coverage. Summing the quantities across those row kinds can double count trade. There are 67 distinct printed unit values including the empty value. [Observation table](../human_trade_observations.tsv.gz) · [Arithmetic checks](../CONSOLIDATION_REPORT.md)

The SITC facet also needs a documented interpretation. For example, 156 headings receive the single string `SITC4_65_84_85_Textiles_Apparel_Footwear`. The property is labeled as a division code although this value names several divisions. Treat the present value as a project rollup and publish individual division mappings only at the precision the evidence supports. The initial graph had four combined headings with only one listed constituent. All eleven combined headings now have at least two. Their composition is more informative, although the source line still gives no allocation weights for splitting its quantity. [Facet and compound assignments](commodities_concepts.jsonl) · [Property declarations](commodities_skos.ttl)

### 6. Document attestation, proposed customs placement, and lexical matches need different treatment

Each document entry has one asserted `oe:subsumedInCustomsHeading` target, spread across 29 customs headings. Abaca illustrates the evidentiary gap. Its cited McCulloch excerpt identifies a Philippine fibre. The record places it under “Esparto and other Vegetable Fibres for making Paper.” The excerpt establishes that the fibre name was in use. It supplies no customs instruction placing abaca imports in that line. Raccoon raises a different question. Its document record proposes “Furs Unenumerated Undressed,” while the register contains “Skins Furs Pelts Racoon” (`htc:0249`, 150 rows). The spelling, product form, period, and jurisdiction deserve comparison before describing it as absent from the ledgers. Record the evidence for each proposed placement and allow competing or unresolved placements. [Document records](document_attested_concepts.jsonl) · [Customs register](htc_id_registry.tsv) · [Gazetteer builder](../../../scripts/build_commodity_gazetteer.py)

A consumer also needs an explicit way to select a tier. In the extended RDF, all 395 document concepts have the literal `document_attested`. Zero registered customs concepts have the corresponding `customs_enumerated` triple. A query filtering for that positive value returns zero rows even though the gazetteer uses it. An absent value also describes hierarchy nodes. Specify which resources qualify and make the two positive filters agree with the published register and document list. [Extended RDF](commodities_skos_extended.ttl) · [Gazetteer](commodity_gazetteer.tsv)

The gazetteer has 3,405 form–concept pairs and 3,243 distinct normalized strings. Eighty-nine strings have multiple candidate IDs. *Linum usitatissimum* has eleven. A mention of flax as a plant cannot by itself identify which processed product or customs heading was meant. Keep the alternatives for retrieval, return the form type with each candidate, and evaluate disambiguation separately from mention detection. For persistent citations, document-tier identifiers are generated from normalized headwords and substance-group identifiers depend in part on the current grounding. Their stability needs a migration policy of its own. [Gazetteer data](commodity_gazetteer.jsonl) · [Gazetteer builder](../../../scripts/build_commodity_gazetteer.py) · [Substance grouping](../../scripts/build_skos_v4.py)

### 7. Some industry links still say more than the cited passage supports

The latest network contains 321 links across 18 industries. Its 575 displayed passages make an editorial review feasible. The remaining problem is deciding precisely which claim a passage supports. For Starch (`htc:0168`) in Chocolate & Confectionery, the sole excerpt describes pastrycooks and confectioners using **dextrine**. It establishes a use of a starch derivative. It gives no application of starch itself as the assigned filler or admixture. For Hops, the direct brewing evidence is convincing, while a different displayed passage describes an unnamed substitute. Counting those passages equally obscures the difference. The regenerated network removed the earlier Sugar Unrefined Total link. That specific defect is closed in the inspected network. [Network evidence](end_use_network_links.jsonl) · [Link decisions](curated_end_use_link_decisions.tsv)

The RDF carries the two directions of the commodity–industry link. The role, quotation, work, and retrieval mode live in the sidecar. A graph consumer cannot select reviewed input roles or qualify a relationship by its source. In the inspected network, 145 links have a single displayed quote and 241 of the 575 quotes point to a Gutenberg ebook landing page. A stable evidence record per assertion would let a reader distinguish an input, derivative, output, or substitute, recover the passage, and see who accepted that reading. Retain the binary relationship for navigation where the reviewed evidence supports it. [Network Turtle](end_use_network_skos.ttl) · [Mining and curation code](../../../scripts/build_end_use_network.py)

### 8. Geographic coverage, validation, and release metadata need clearer boundaries

The place tables distinguish 330 single-place matches, 11 compound matches, 14 explicit aggregates, and 144 unresolved names. That is a sound distinction. For example, “Montreal and Quebec” has two component identifiers and 1,621 mentions. Expanding it into two full trade observations would double the reported flow. The 93.9% figure covers place **mentions** matched to one or more QIDs. The polity table answers a narrower question. It contains 1,067 distinct printed-place/start-year pairs, against 6,885 observed pairs for names carrying a single QID under the builder’s row filter. Political membership need only apply to part of that denominator. An omitted pair currently gives a consumer no reason, and lookup for a Canadian port with a province uses the Canada QID. Publish the applicable time span, lookup basis, and reasons such as outside coverage, unresolved, or invalid year. [Place records](places_grounding.jsonl) · [Polity records](place_year_polities.tsv) · [Resolution logic](../../scripts/ground_places_v2.py)

The revised extended audit counts repeated preferred labels across nodes within each scheme and calls them S14 violations. It now avoids cross-scheme false alarms. S14 concerns a *single resource* having more than one preferred label in a language. “Bark” on two resources would still be legal if they belonged to the same scheme. Keeping labels distinct within a browsing scheme is a reasonable editorial policy with a different test. The audit also scans Turtle text for blank-node syntax and inspects compact JSON IDs. Parsed graphs make it possible to test the actual RDF terms and the cross-file joins that matter here. [Extended audit](../../../scripts/audit_extended_skos.py) · [SKOS lexical integrity conditions](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/#S14)

Two release details matter for curation. The commodity review queue labels 750 headings as local-only. Of those, 171 now have external anchors and 60 current local-only headings are missing. Separately, the core scheme carries version `4.1-lincs-compatible`, the mapping set uses `/v4`, and the repository names the content review v6. These could legitimately be different version dimensions. A manifest tying the data release, schema version, curation revision, enriched authority data, and derived artifacts together would make a cited result reproducible. The builder’s fixed filesystem paths, private 1866 workbook, live enrichment on a cache miss, and use of the current date make a fresh build sensitive to its environment. A public verification path can operate entirely on published extracts and hashes while preserving the source restrictions. [Review queue](commodities_review_queue.tsv) · [Core builder](../../scripts/build_skos_v4.py) · [Source policy](../../../SOURCES.md)

The following read-only checks can be repeated on a later release using RDFLib from the repository root.

```python
from pathlib import Path
from rdflib import Graph, Namespace, SKOS

folder = Path("human_trade/layer1_human/grounding")
graphs = {}
for turtle in sorted(folder.glob("*.ttl")):
    graph = Graph().parse(turtle, format="turtle")
    jsonld = Graph().parse(turtle.with_suffix(".jsonld"), format="json-ld")
    print(turtle.stem, len(graph), len(graph - jsonld), len(jsonld - graph))
    graphs[turtle.stem] = graph

core = graphs["commodities_skos"]
extended = graphs["commodities_skos_extended"]
network = graphs["end_use_network_skos"]
scheme = Namespace("https://open-empire.hgislab.usask.ca/vocab/human-trade-commodities/").scheme
# Read the predicate namespace declared by the network under test.
industry = Namespace(dict(network.namespaces())["oe"])
inputs = set(network.subjects(industry.usedInIndustry, None))
members = set(extended.subjects(SKOS.inScheme, scheme))
print("Core triples missing from extension", len(core - extended))
print("Network commodities and joined commodities", len(inputs), len(inputs & members))
```

For the date findings, filter the compressed observations on the two cited record IDs and compare the printed year, parsed year, and QA flags with the Flax Seeds concept and Quebec polity rows. For the period test, group commodity-bearing rows by their registered heading, exclude QA and blank rows, and compare each concept’s last attestation with the maximum numeric end year. These checks preserve the historical evidence while exposing exactly where a derived assertion changes its meaning.
